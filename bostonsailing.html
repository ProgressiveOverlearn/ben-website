Here Are Recommended Locations For Sailing in Boston:

⛵Community Boating
21 David G Mugar Way, Boston, MA 02114

Click Here For Their Website

⭐Classes are offered for adults and children
⭐Variety of membership offerings for sailors of all skill levels
⭐Relatively low costs for sailing
⭐Sailboat rentals also available for non-members

⛵Courageous Sailing
Navy Yard, Pier Four Blvd, Boston, MA 02129

Click Here For Their Website

⭐Classes are offered for adults and children
⭐Youth programming offers a path for youth to go from being a beginner to instructor
⭐Nearby parking at the Nautica Garage with low cost
⭐Courses are available, and require no membership

⛵Piers Park Sailing Center
95 Marginal St, Boston, MA 02128

Click Here For Their Website

⭐Classes are offered for adults and children
⭐Sailing afterschool program offered for ages 10+
⭐Cold water sailing offered through the Hardy Sailor Season Pass
⭐Learn To Sail class and Sailing Season Pass can be bought in a bundle

⛵SailTime Boston
1 Pier 8, Charlestown, MA 02129

Click Here For Their Website

⭐Classes are offered for adults only
⭐Small class sizes of 2 to 4 students for 1 instructor
⭐Premier membership allows you to take your boat out for up to 7 continous days
⭐Six ASA courses are available: 101, 103, 104, 105, 106, & 114

⛵Crimson Sailing Academy
45 Memorial Dr, Cambridge, MA 02142

Click Here For Their Website

⭐Classes are offered for children only
⭐A clear and detailed schedule for campers in the program
⭐Campers must pass a swimming test, but the test is free
⭐One week registration is $600 instead of $650 if done early

⛵Boston Sailing Center
54 Lewis Wharf, Boston, MA 02110

Click Here For Their Website

⭐Classes are offered for adults only
⭐Variety of memberships available for unlimited sailing
⭐Can "share" memberships to 2 other friends for 50% off of your own membership
⭐Beginner and advanced sailing courses available
